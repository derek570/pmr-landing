ABOUT US

BUILT BY BIKERS.

Built on experience, precision and a genuine passion for motorcycles.

This business was born from a lifelong passion for motorcycles and a desire to provide a transport service that riders can genuinely trust.

Before starting the business, I worked as a medical engineer, having completed an MSc Honours in Medical Electronics and Physics. I also led a team of engineers, where precision, attention to detail, safety and clear communication were essential every day. Those experiences have shaped the way I work and are principles I bring to the business today.

My passion for motorcycles has also taken me into the world of professional racing. I have had the opportunity to work with a British Superbike race team, as well as competing in club racing myself. This has given me first-hand experience of the racing environment and a genuine understanding of the commitment, excitement and passion that riders have for their motorcycles.

I understand that a motorcycle is more than just transport. It can be a significant investment, a passion and something that its owner takes enormous pride in.

YOUR BIKE, OUR CARE.

When you entrust us with your motorcycle, we understand the responsibility that comes with it. We treat every bike as if it were our own, taking care throughout collection, loading, transport and delivery. From the moment your motorcycle is collected until it reaches its destination, you can expect careful handling, clear communication and a professional service you can rely on.

That’s why we provide a careful, professional and personal motorcycle transport service. Whether you’re travelling to a track day, attending a race meeting, buying or selling a motorcycle, or simply need your bike transported safely, our aim is to provide a reliable service and complete peace of mind.
